Regional Favoritism

A Replication and Extension of Hodler & Raschky (2014)

Ömer Furkan Çoban · Carl von Ossietzky University of Oldenburg

Hodler and Raschky (2014, QJE) find that a region grows brighter at night while it is the birth region of the sitting political leader: evidence of regional favoritism. This site hosts the paper, its supplementary materials, and the accompanying presentation, all built on an independently constructed replication pipeline extended through 2023.
